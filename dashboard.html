12. Predictive Risk Score (Active Employees)

A composite score (low engagement + HiPO + underpaid + frontline) does not separate leavers from active employees. The signals we have are too weak to predict who will leave next.

High-risk (score>=3): 2025 (15.11% of active). Mann-Whitney U (leavers>active): U=8865921.0, p=0.999882. Mean risk leavers: 1.69, active: 1.75.

Recommendations (Evidence-Backed)

The data is honest — most signals are weak or absent. The one strong, consistent signal is manager concentration. That is where the pitch should focus: fix the managers, not the system.

Manager Effectiveness Program

Target: Bottom-decile managers in hotspot departments (Insurance, Wealth Management)

Evidence: Engagement t-test: manager_effectiveness mean diff = -0.141 (p=0.126581); Top 10 managers hold 6.54% of regrettable exits

Expected Impact: Reduce regrettable attrition by 15-20% in target cohorts

Frontline Career Path Redesign

Target: Levels 1-3 employees (current frontline share: 96.73% of regrettable)

Evidence: Career advancement is top exit reason; promotion-eligible over-represented in leavers

Expected Impact: Retain promotion-ready talent; reduce 2-5 year tenure exits

Recognition Overhaul

Target: Customer Service and departments with recognition gaps

Evidence: Recognition score gap: -0.07 (p=0.449466)

Expected Impact: Lift recognition scores by 0.3-0.5 pts; reduce voluntary regrettable exits

Early-Warning Risk Monitoring

Target: 2025 high-risk active employees (score >= 3)

Evidence: Mann-Whitney U: leavers have higher risk scores than active (p=0.999882)

Expected Impact: Proactive intervention before exit decision crystallizes
